OpenUBEM

Creator & Developer

Open-source Urban Building Energy Modeling Platform

Give it a neighbourhood (address, coordinate, bounding box, or OpenStreetMap export) and it estimates the annual energy use and carbon emissions of every building. Each building is mapped to one of 30 DOE archetypes and simulated as its own full-year EnergyPlus model. Validated against measured data on 8,160 buildings in New York, Los Angeles, and Austin.

  • Python
  • EnergyPlus
  • OpenStreetMap
